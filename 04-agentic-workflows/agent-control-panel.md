## What Juno Is Doing

Insight Synthesis > 3 – Semi autonomous. ReAct, monitor if it fails to generate the report (connectivity failures). One-Agent loop

## Four Levers

**Stop Conditions** (Loop):

max_steps: 3. Abort if same tool fails 2x in a row. Hard timeout: 90s wall clock.

**Structured Tool Outputs** (Tools):

Read tools: get roadmap items. get capacity snapshop, get pipeline accounts, get signals feed.
Synthesis tools: classify signal, detect conflct against commited tasks, summarise signals, map to roadmap.
Write tools: draft ticket, flag for escalation, update raw signal status.

**Confidence Thresholds** (Verification):

High confidence auto-posts to #pm-daily. Medium confidence posts to #pm-juno-review for a reviewer first. Low confidence requires PM approval before anything goes out.

**North Star** (Context):

RocketShip Product Strategy One-Pager: Defines the priority framework (P0–P3) Juno will use to reason about every signal it encounters. 
Engineering Capacity Note: Establishes hard constraints. Understand what engineering cannot take on, not just what's planned.
Product Quarterly Roadmap: The committed vs. uncommitted split.
Raw Signals: Raw input Juno is presumably processing. The signals to do anything useful.
Sales Pipeline Snapshot: Surfaces business risk (Pearson at-risk renewal, Acme hard blocker). Influence how Juno weights urgency.

## Rules of Engagement

**Agency Permission:**

Escalate p0 tickets
Update raw signals status

**Access Control:**

READ: Slack #escalations, Strategy KB, JIRA
ARR. WRITE: #pm-daily only, Jira stubs only. CANNOT edit Salesforce, or post outside #pm-daily.

**Fallback Protocols:**

After 3 failed retrievals, degrade to "cautious mode" (no priorities, just thread links). After 2 tool errors, escalate to PM with full trace.

**Checkpoints:**

Any thread mentioning "churn", "legal", or "security" requires PM approval. Any P0 where Juno is low confidence goes to PM review.

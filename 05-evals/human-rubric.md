# Human Evaluation Rubric · Juno

_Module 5. Generated by `M5 - Human Evaluation Rubric.html`. Paste over `05-evals/human-rubric.md`._

## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno’s weekly insights synthesis
- **Value proposition:** Turn the week’s customer insights and team signals into the top three risks I need to act on, each with a source I can check.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy (deal-breaker) | Does each risk come from something that actually happened in the source material?  | Yes / No | At least one risk is invented or can’t be traced.  | n/a | Every risk traces to a real message, ticket, or doc. |
| Sources | Does every risk link to the message or ticket it came from? | Yes / No | One or more risks has no source, or the link is wrong. If it cannot find a clear rule to support a ticket, it stops and says “I do not have enough evidence to decide” and routes to a human. It never produces a better-sounding citation to pass the gate. Cautious beats guessing, and an unverified determination is surfaced labelled, never quietly dropped. | n/a | Always show the Slack ticket ID or Jira ticket key for every claim you make |
| Prioritization | Are the three risks the ones that most deserve my attention this week? | 1 to 5 | Misses an obvious critical risk, or ranks a minor one first. | Covers the critical risks, but the order needs fixing. | Matches the risks I would have picked, in the order I would have picked them. |
| Usefulness | Could I act on this summary without rereading the sources? | 1 to 5 | I have to reread everything to know what to do. | Clear enough to act on, but I still check one or two sources. | I can act on it straight away and forward it to my team.  |
| Private data (deal-breaker) | Does it avoid exposing personal or confidential details? | Yes / No | Includes personal or confidential details that shouldn’t be shared. | n/a | No names, salaries, or customer details beyond what’s needed. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** 10 summaries a week, including the ones I felt least sure about
- **Who scores them with you:** Me, plus one other PM once a month
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** If our scores differ by 2 or more on any question, we talk it through and rewrite that description so it’s clearer next time.

## Pass bar

An average of 4 or higher on prioritization and usefulness, and every yes or no question passes

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Accuracy: any "no"
- Private data: any "no"
